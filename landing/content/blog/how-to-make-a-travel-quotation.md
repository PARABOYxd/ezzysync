A client sends a WhatsApp message: "Goa, 4 nights, 2 adults, mid-December, what will it cost?" What you send back in the next few hours decides most of whether you get the booking. Not the destination knowledge in your head, not your years in the business, but the quotation itself: how fast it arrives, how clear it is, and whether the client can trust the number.

This guide covers **how to make a travel quotation** from the first question to the follow-up, with a format you can copy and a fully worked pricing example in rupees.

## Quotation, itinerary and invoice: three different documents

Agents often mix these up, and clients notice.

| Document | When you send it | What it answers | Is it binding? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Itinerary** | With or before the quote | "What will I do each day?" | No |
| **Quotation** | After you know the requirements | "What will it cost, and what is included?" | It is an offer, valid until a date you set |
| **Invoice** | After the client confirms and pays an advance | "What do I owe, and what tax is charged?" | Yes, it is a tax document |

A good quotation carries a short day-wise summary of the itinerary inside it, so the client sees the price and what they get for it on the same page. The full itinerary can follow as a separate link or PDF.

## Step 1: Collect the requirements before you price

A fast quote built on a guess gets rejected. A quote built on five good questions gets compared seriously. Before you open your rate sheets, make sure you have:

- **Destination and route.** "Kerala" is not enough. Munnar, Thekkady and Alleppey, or just Kochi and Alleppey?
- **Dates or month of travel.** Rates in peak weeks (Christmas to New Year, Diwali, long weekends) can be very different from the week before.
- **Number of travellers and their ages.** Children's ages decide extra-bed and child-without-bed charges.
- **Hotel category.** Ask "3-star, 4-star or a specific resort you have in mind?" rather than "what is your budget?"
- **Budget range.** Ask it gently: "So I can suggest the right hotels, roughly what range are you planning for?"
- **Must-haves.** Honeymoon decoration, a houseboat night, a specific beach, vegetarian meals, a senior citizen who cannot climb stairs.
- **Departure city.** Even if you are not booking flights, it changes the arrival day plan.

A simple way to do this on WhatsApp is to send one message with these questions as a numbered list. Clients reply faster to a list than to a back-and-forth chat. Save the answers against the lead, not only in the chat, so anyone in your team can price it. ## Step 2: Price the package (with a worked example)

Pricing is where most agents lose either the deal or their margin. The method below keeps both.

### Build the net cost first

List every cost you will pay to suppliers, for the whole group, before adding anything for yourself.

Worked example: **Goa, 4 nights / 5 days, 2 adults, mid-December, 4-star hotel.**

| Cost item | Calculation | Net cost |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel, deluxe room with breakfast | 4 nights × ₹6,500 | ₹26,000 |
| Airport pickup and drop | 2 transfers | ₹3,000 |
| North Goa and South Goa sightseeing by private cab | 2 days | ₹4,500 |
| Evening river cruise | 2 tickets × ₹1,000 | ₹2,000 |
| Candle-light dinner (honeymoon add-on) | 1 evening | ₹2,000 |
| **Total net cost** | | **₹37,500** |

### Add your margin as a rupee amount

Decide your margin in rupees for the trip, not as a vague "some markup". Think about the work involved: a 5-day domestic package with one hotel needs less of your time than a 10-day multi-city trip with three hotels and a train in between.

In this example, the agent adds **₹6,000** as margin.

- Package price before tax: ₹37,500 + ₹6,000 = **₹43,500**
- Per person (2 adults): **₹21,750**, plus GST as applicable

### Check the number against the market

Before you send it, ask two questions:

1. **Would I pay this?** If a client can find the same hotel on a booking site for much less, show what they get that the site does not include: transfers, sightseeing, a person to call when the cab is late.
2. **Is there room to adjust?** Keep a lower option ready (a different hotel, or sightseeing on a shared coach) instead of cutting your margin when the client asks for a discount.

### Offer two or three options, not one

A single price invites a yes-or-no decision. Two or three options invite the client to choose between them.

| Option | Hotel | Price for 2 (before tax) |
|---|---|---|
| Value | 3-star near Calangute | ₹34,000 |
| **Recommended** | 4-star with pool | **₹43,500** |
| Premium | 5-star beach resort | ₹68,000 |

Mark one option as recommended. Most clients pick it, because you have made the choice easy.

## Step 3: The travel quotation format

A quotation should be readable on a phone in under two minutes, because that is where your client will open it. Use this order.

### Header

- Your agency name, logo, address, phone and GSTIN
- Quote number and date (for example `Q-2026-118`, 16 Nov 2026)
- Client name and phone
- **Valid until** date. Hotel rates change, so 3 to 7 days is common in peak season.

### Trip summary

One short block the client can read at a glance:

> **Goa Honeymoon, 4 Nights / 5 Days**
> 15 to 19 December 2026 · 2 Adults · 4-star hotel with breakfast

### Day-wise plan

Two or three lines per day are enough in the quote:

- **Day 1:** Arrive Goa, private pickup, check in. Evening free at the beach.
- **Day 2:** North Goa sightseeing: Fort Aguada, Baga and Anjuna. Evening river cruise.
- **Day 3:** South Goa sightseeing: Old Goa churches, Colva beach.
- **Day 4:** Day at leisure. Candle-light dinner at the hotel.
- **Day 5:** Check out, drop at the airport.

If you want to build these quickly, the [free itinerary builder](/free-itinerary-builder) creates a day-wise plan you can edit, with no login.

### Hotels

Name the hotel, room type and meal plan, with "or similar" only if you genuinely may change it. Clients compare hotel names online, so a named hotel builds trust.

### Price

Show the price the way the client thinks about it:

- Package price for 2 adults
- Per person price
- GST shown as its own line
- Total payable

Do not list your net costs or your margin. The client is buying a trip, not auditing your supplier rates.

### Inclusions and exclusions

This section prevents most disputes after the trip. Be specific.

**Inclusions:** accommodation as listed with breakfast, private airport transfers, sightseeing by private AC cab as per the plan, river cruise tickets, candle-light dinner, driver allowance, tolls and parking.

**Exclusions:** flights or trains, lunch and dinner unless stated, entry tickets at monuments, water sports, personal expenses, anything not listed under inclusions.

### Payment terms

- Advance to confirm the booking (state the rupee amount, for example ₹15,000)
- Balance due date (for example, 15 days before travel)
- Accepted payment modes and your bank or UPI details

### Cancellation policy

Write it in plain words with dates: what is refundable if the client cancels 30 days before, 15 days before and within 7 days of travel. Say clearly that hotel and transport cancellation charges follow the supplier's policy in peak season.

### Next step

End with one clear action: "Reply YES on WhatsApp to hold these hotels. We will send the payment link and confirmation."

## Step 4: GST on travel quotations, the basics

GST is where quotations most often go wrong, and a wrong tax line looks careless to a client. A few rules of thumb:

- **Show GST on the quote.** A quote that says "plus taxes" without a number makes the client wonder what the final amount will be.
- **Use the right SAC code.** Tour operator services use **SAC 998555**.
- **Know which way you bill.** A tour operator selling a complete package charges GST on the package value. An agent who books only as a pure agent for the client (passing costs through at actual) charges GST on the service fee. The two are taxed differently.
- **Confirm the current rate with your CA.** Rates and conditions, including whether you can claim input tax credit, change from time to time. Set them up once with your accountant and use the same treatment on every quote.

## Step 5: Send the quote and follow up

### Speed matters more than polish

A clear quote sent the same day usually beats a beautiful quote sent three days later. Build templates for your top destinations (Goa, Kerala, Kashmir, Rajasthan, Dubai, Thailand) so that a new quote is mostly editing dates and hotels.

### Send it where the client already talks to you

If the enquiry came on WhatsApp, send the quote on WhatsApp: a mobile link plus a PDF for those who like to forward it to family. Add one line of context:

> Hi Priya, here is your Goa honeymoon quote for 15 to 19 December. I have marked the 4-star option as recommended. These hotel rates are held until Friday. Any questions, just reply here.

Our guide to [WhatsApp marketing for travel agents](/blog/whatsapp-marketing-for-travel-agents) covers message templates in more detail.

### Follow up on a schedule, not on memory

Most quotes do not get a reply on the first day. That is normal; the client is checking with family or comparing. A simple follow-up rhythm:

| When | What to send |
|---|---|
| Day 1 (next morning) | "Did you get a chance to look at the quote? Happy to change hotels or dates." |
| Day 3 | Something useful: weather in December, a photo of the hotel pool, a note that the rate is held until Friday. |
| Before the validity date | "Your rate is held until tomorrow. Shall I block the rooms?" |
| After it expires | "Rates have changed slightly. Shall I send a fresh quote for the same dates?" |

Set a follow-up date on every lead the moment you send a quote. ### Track what happened to each quote

Mark each quote as sent, accepted or lost, with a reason for lost quotes ("price", "dates changed", "booked elsewhere"). After a month you will see a pattern, such as losing most Kashmir quotes on hotel price, and you can fix the cause instead of guessing.

## Common mistakes that lose bookings

**Sending a price without inclusions.** "₹43,500 for Goa" invites comparison with every other number the client has seen. Inclusions explain why your number is what it is.

**No validity date.** Without one, a client comes back six weeks later expecting the same rate after the hotel has raised it.

**Hiding GST until the invoice.** The client feels misled, even if the tax is correct.

**One option only.** The client either accepts or goes quiet. Two or three options keep the conversation going.

**Unnamed hotels.** "4-star hotel in Calangute" sounds like you have not decided yet.

**Quotes in five different formats.** If every staff member uses their own Word file, the agency looks less reliable. One template, one letterhead.

**No follow-up.** A quote without a follow-up is a quote you have given to the next agency.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should a travel quotation include?

Your agency details and GSTIN, a quote number and validity date, the client's name, a trip summary, a short day-wise plan, named hotels with room type and meal plan, the price with GST shown separately, inclusions and exclusions, payment terms, cancellation policy and a clear next step.

### How long should a travel quotation be valid?

In peak season, 3 to 7 days is common because hotel rates move quickly. Off-season you can give longer. State the date clearly, and say that rooms are not blocked until the advance is paid.

### Should I show my margin or net costs in the quote?

No. Show the package price, the per-person price, GST and the total. Your supplier rates and margin are internal. If a client asks for a breakdown, offer the hotel and transport components, not your cost sheet.

### How fast should I send a quotation?

The same day if you can. A clear quote sent within a few hours, with one recommended option, usually beats a detailed one sent days later. Destination templates make same-day quotes realistic even in busy weeks.

### Can I make travel quotations in Excel or Word?

Yes, many agencies start that way. It gets hard when several staff send quotes, when you need GST worked out the same way every time, or when you want to know which quotes were accepted.
