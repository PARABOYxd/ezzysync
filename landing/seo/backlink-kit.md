### Medium description (~60 words)

```
EzzySync is a cloud travel CRM built for Indian travel agencies, tour operators, DMCs and independent travel consultants. It brings WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries into one lead pipeline, reminds your team about follow-ups, builds day-wise itineraries, and produces GST quotations and invoices. Bookings, hotel vouchers and payments are tracked in the same place. 30-day free trial, no credit card.
```

### Long description (~200 words)

```
EzzySync is a cloud travel CRM for Indian travel agencies, tour operators, DMCs and independent travel consultants. It runs in the browser on a laptop or phone, with nothing to install.

Enquiries from WhatsApp (including click-to-WhatsApp ads), Instagram DMs and your website form are captured automatically as leads. Other leads can be added manually or imported from Excel/CSV. Each lead carries its source, destination, budget and assigned agent, and moves through a drag-and-drop Kanban pipeline. Follow-up dates put every lead in front of the right agent on the day it is due, with one-tap call, WhatsApp and email.

The day-wise itinerary builder covers sightseeing, hotel stays, transfers and flights, with inclusions and exclusions. An AI generator turns a destination, number of days and travel style into an editable draft. Quotations calculate GST (tour operator SAC 998555) and per-person pricing, and are shared as a mobile web link or a PDF on your letterhead.

Accepted itineraries become bookings with hotel vouchers, driver duty slips and group departure batches. Advances and instalments are recorded per booking, GST invoices are generated as PDFs, and Razorpay payments are matched to bookings.

WhatsApp runs on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API with your own business number and a shared team inbox.

Plans start at ₹999 per month, with a 30-day free trial and no credit card required.
```
