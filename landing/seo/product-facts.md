# EzzySync — product facts for generated content

The weekly SEO job gives this file to the AI writer as the ONLY source of
truth about the product. If a feature is not listed here, generated content
must not claim it. Update this file when the product changes.

## What it is
- EzzySync is a cloud travel CRM for Indian travel agencies, tour operators, DMCs and independent travel consultants.
- Web app, works on laptop and phone browser. Nothing to install.
- Website: https://www.ezzysync.com — free trial, no credit card.

## Plans (monthly, INR)
- Solo Agent — ₹999/month: 1 login, up to 200 inquiries, day-wise itinerary builder with PDF, quotations and invoices, follow-up reminders, shared EzzySync WhatsApp number.
- Agency Growth — ₹2,499/month: up to 5 team logins, unlimited leads and bookings, your own number on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, team inbox on one shared number, AI itinerary generator, Razorpay payments matched to bookings, your letterhead on quotes and PDFs.
- Enterprise & DMCs — custom pricing, billed annually: unlimited logins and roles, multiple branches, custom WhatsApp workflows, help migrating Excel/CSV data, dedicated onboarding.
- 30-day free trial with full Pro access. Cancel or switch plans any time.

## Leads and pipeline
- Captured automatically: WhatsApp messages (including from click-to-WhatsApp ads), Instagram DMs, and the agency's website form.
- Added manually or by Excel/CSV import: everything else (walk-ins, phone calls, referrals, Facebook lead forms, Google Ads). The lead's source can be tagged.
- There is NO direct integration with Facebook/Meta lead-ad forms or Google Ads lead forms.
- Each lead has source, destination, budget and an assigned agent.
- Kanban pipeline with drag-and-drop stages (e.g. New, Contacted, Requirements gathered, Won, Lost) and total value per stage.
- Filter by agent, customer, phone, package or source. Reassign leads.

## Follow-ups
- Set a follow-up date on any lead; on the due day it appears in the follow-up list.
- Buckets: priority today, new leads, pending leads, no action taken.
- One-tap call, WhatsApp (without saving the number) and email shortcuts. Mark done.
- The agent sends follow-up messages; EzzySync reminds and opens the chat. It does not auto-send follow-ups.

## WhatsApp
- Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API with your own business number (Agency Growth), shared team inbox, chats linked to leads.
- Solo users can start on EzzySync's shared number or link WhatsApp by QR code.
- Share itinerary links, quote PDFs and invoices to the client's WhatsApp.
- EzzySync adds no per-message markup; Meta's own conversation charges apply to Cloud API numbers.

## Itineraries and quotations
- Day-wise itinerary builder: sightseeing, hotel stays, transfers and flights per day; inclusions and exclusions.
- Duplicate any itinerary to reuse it.
- Quotes calculate GST (tour operator SAC 998555) and totals; per-person price for groups.
- Share as a mobile web link or a PDF on your letterhead; track whether the client accepted.
- AI itinerary generator (Agency Growth): destination, days and travel style in, editable day-wise draft out.
- A free itinerary builder on the website needs no login: https://www.ezzysync.com/free-itinerary-builder

## Hotels, bookings, operations
- Hotel inventory: properties, room categories, cost vs selling rates, multiple contacts (sales, reservations).
- Bookings created from an accepted itinerary; hotel and room per stay; pending/confirmed status and voucher number per hotel; driver duty slips.
- Upcoming trips view by departure date.
- Tour batches for fixed group departures: seat capacity, seats filled, linked bookings.
- Expenses per trip or general, by category; trip costing templates.

## Payments and invoices
- Record advance and instalments per booking; see balance due.
- GST invoices with custom invoice prefix and footer terms; download PDF, send by email or WhatsApp.
- Razorpay payments matched to bookings on Agency Growth.

## Team and data
- Staff logins with per-login permissions. Reassign a staff member's leads before removing them.
- Each agency's data is isolated from other accounts.
- Bookings export to CSV.
- Dashboard: bookings, monthly revenue, pending payments.

## Do NOT claim
- Any ranking or award ("#1", "best rated", "top rated"), customer counts, reviews or testimonials.
- Statistics or percentages (market size, conversion lift, time saved) — none are verified.
- TCS calculation, customer-list export, automatic follow-up messages, a mobile app from an app store, integrations not listed above (Tally, Zoho, Salesforce, flight/hotel booking APIs, GDS).
